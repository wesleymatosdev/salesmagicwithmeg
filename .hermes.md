# Sales Magic with Meg — client site

One-page branded website for Meghann Misiak ("That Sales Witch 🔮"), sales coach.

## Facts
- Live: https://salesmagicwithmeg.pages.dev (Cloudflare Pages project `salesmagicwithmeg`, direct-upload deploy)
- Source: this repo, static files only (index.html, 404.html, favicon.svg, og-image.png) — no build step
- Deploy: `npx wrangler@4.168.0 pages deploy . --project-name=salesmagicwithmeg --branch=main` (uses CLOUDFLARE_API_KEY/ACCOUNT_ID env)
- Brand: deep purple mystic palette (bg #12041f, card #22103d, accent #a855f7), Manrope font, inline SVG icon sprite (no icon CDN)
- Booking: standalone branded booking page at https://demo.wmatos.dev/meg-calendly/ (sites-monorepo apps/demo/meg-calendly, PRs sites#16/#17). Plain fixed-height iframe on her direct Calendly URL with theme color params — NOT widget.js (its postMessage resize stalls at ~150px with third-party-cookie blocking, see PR #17). Sun/moon toggle swaps iframe src; dark default; `meg-theme` localStorage; fallback direct link under the calendar. Main site's #book section links here. On real publish: move to salesmagicwithmeg.com/book and repoint the CTA. Calendly remains the booking engine (self-host dropped)
- Content sources: her LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/megmisiak), current Wix site (salesmagicwithmeg.com), Calendly event copy. All facts real (75+ sellers, Deel/HubSpot/Salesforce/Blend/Movable Ink, Neha Singh testimonial from her Wix site)
- Copy: variation of her real Wix voice — hero "F*ck hustle culture.", "Let's regulate the humans behind your revenue", her titles (Sales Coach · Revenue Strategist · Energy Witch), newsletter "Letters to My Burned Out Self". Main site is dark-only (light theme removed)
- Her Wix site still live at salesmagicwithmeg.com; DNS/Wix migration NOT done — Meg should review the pages.dev demo first

## Gotchas
- Cloudflare Pages returns 308 (not 404) for /404.html direct hit; the custom 404 serves fine on bad routes
- Cloud browser sessions get bot-walled by calendly.com pages, but the embed iframe loads fine on the deployed site
