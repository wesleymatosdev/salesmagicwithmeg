About Meg

Hi, I’m Meg Misiak 🔮

Sales Coach · Revenue Strategist · Energy Witch

After a decade scaling sales orgs, I burned out. Hard. I used to believe success meant staying later, pushing harder, and sacrificing everything for the number on the board. A bigger paycheck and President’s Club were supposed to finally make me happy. They didn’t.

So I spent the last five years rethinking everything I knew about success, sales, and the corporate ladder. Now I work with sellers who are doing really well on paper but deeply, existentially exhausted. The ones at the top of the leaderboard wondering why they don’t feel proud.

I’ve coached 75+ sellers from teams at Deel, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Blend to hit top performance without the burnout: deal scorecards and qualification frameworks, founders learning to sell their own product without feeling gross about it, and the tools to regulate your nervous system so you close your laptop earlier and still hit #1.

Effortless Abundance is the sweet spot where strategy meets nervous system regulation. Results feel sustainable. Success stops costing you your wellbeing.

The through-line

You can win more with less effort.

Tactical, client-centric sales strategies that make selling effective and fulfilling. A mantra of effort-LESS abundance. And the tools to get your life, hobbies, and stress back under control.

My clients hit #1 on their team, get promotions, and become legends at their company. They also write books, speak on stages, and launch the creative projects they didn’t think they had space for.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who do you work with?

Burned-out, top-performing SaaS sellers and founders who are doing well on paper but existentially exhausted. Also sales teams, through bootcamps, training, and Energy Audits.

Is this actual sales strategy or just mindset woo?

Both, on purpose. You get deal scorecards, qualification frameworks, discovery through negotiation, and pipeline reviews, combined with the nervous system and mindset work that removes the root-cause blocks killing your performance. The strategy makes you effective. The regulation makes it sustainable.

What happens in the free consult?

We dig into your goals (personal and professional, short and long term), your challenges (known issues, potential blind spots, anything holding you back), and the support you need right now and in what format. You leave with clarity either way, and there is zero pressure to buy.

Do you work with teams and companies?

Yes. Consulting and training for teams, The Energy Audit for kickoffs and offsites, and four-week bootcamps. For team inquiries, email meg@salesmagicwithmeg.com or mention it in your consult booking.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I’m based in Mexico City and coach sellers and founders around the world. Sessions run over video, and Energy Audits travel for on-site events.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on the format and what you need, and it’s shared openly on the consult. Booking the consult itself is free.

Can we partner on content or brand deals?

I’m open to brand partnerships. Reach out at meg@salesmagicwithmeg.com with what you have in mind.
